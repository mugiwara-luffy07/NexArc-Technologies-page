Most small business websites fail for the same reason: a visitor lands, cannot tell what the business does or who it is for, and leaves. We start from that problem.

## What we focus on

- **Clear first screen.** What you do, who it is for and how to contact you, before anyone scrolls.
- **Speed on real phones.** Most of your visitors are on mid-range Android phones over mobile data. We build and test for that.
- **Found on Google.** Clean structure, proper metadata and local business details so people nearby can find you.
- **Enquiries that reach you.** Forms go straight to your email and phone, not into a dashboard you never open.
