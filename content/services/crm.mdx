A CRM only helps if people actually use it. So we sit with the team first, watch how bookings and follow-ups really happen, and build software that matches.

## How it usually looks

- **Quick entry.** Capture a booking in the words you heard it, not across fifteen form fields.
- **What needs attention today.** Pending payments, unconfirmed bookings and upcoming events, listed for you.
- **One tap actions.** Call, WhatsApp confirmation, payment reminder and map directions straight from a record.
