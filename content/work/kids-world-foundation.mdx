## The problem

The foundation runs regular events and programmes for children and needed a website that stays current. Every update, from a new event to a photo gallery, had to be easy for their own team to publish.

## What we built

- **Public site** covering the foundation's story, upcoming and past events, photo galleries, videos and records.
- **Event pages** with venue and timing details.
- **Admin panel** behind a login, so the team adds events, photos and videos themselves.
- **Content in Supabase**, so everything the team publishes appears on the site straight away.
