## The problem

SSS Catering takes most of its bookings over the phone, for weddings and functions fixed by the Tamil calendar. Details were written in notebooks: the Tamil date, the hall, the headcount, the advance paid. Checking whether a date was free meant flipping pages, and payment follow-ups depended on memory.

## What we built

A CRM shaped around how the owner already works.

- **Type the booking the way you heard it.** A line like "Purattasi 11 marriage Ravi, Sri Lakshmi Mahal Erode, 750 people, 2.5L, adv 50k" fills the whole booking form.
- **Tamil date first.** Pick the Tamil month and day; the English date shows underneath. Booked days are marked, and a same-day clash shows a warning.
- **Remembers halls.** Choose a hall used before and the town, address and map link fill in.
- **Money in plain words.** Type 2.5L, 50k or 1,20,000. Rate times headcount fills the total and the balance is worked out.
- **One tap actions.** Call, WhatsApp confirmation, payment reminder, directions and calendar entries from any booking.
- **Needs attention.** No advance yet, still tentative, balance due this week, event tomorrow. Listed every morning.

## How it is built

Next.js on Vercel with a Supabase database, email and password sign-in limited to approved staff, and automated tests for the Tamil calendar, the booking parser and the full booking flow on phone and laptop browsers.
